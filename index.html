Mr. Zahir Shah

Senior Member Board of Revenue — Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

The Revenue & Estate Department, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa has undertaken a transformative initiative for the computerization of land records across the province, aimed at improving service delivery and enhancing public convenience. Under this initiative, modern technologies are being incorporated into the land record management system to ensure efficiency, transparency, and accuracy.

The integration of digitized land records into a centralized database will enable real-time updates from a single platform and allow seamless connectivity with various service gateways. At the same time, manual settlement of land records continues in several districts to ensure completeness and reliability of data.

In addition, a GIS-based land settlement approach has recently been introduced in seven sub-divisions of the newly merged districts to develop fully digitized and spatially accurate records. A similar settlement initiative is also underway in the districts of Lower Dir, Upper Dir, and Sub-Tehsil Kalam in District Swat.

About Planning Cell

Planning Cell
Revenue & Estate Department, KP

The Planning Cell, Revenue & Estate Department is a dedicated institutional unit established under the Board of Revenue, Government of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Its core mandate is to oversee, plan, coordinate, and monitor all developmental schemes undertaken by the District Administrations across the 26 districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

In pursuance of the Rules of Business of the Government of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, the Planning Cell operates as the principal planning authority within the Revenue & Estate Department, interfacing with district-level administrations, the Board of Revenue, and other provincial departments to ensure aligned and effective public investment.

The Cell is responsible for the preparation, appraisal, approval, execution oversight, and evaluation of developmental schemes — ensuring transparency, fiscal discipline, and delivery of public services to citizens across KP. It also coordinates with the Planning Commission of Pakistan and development partners including ADB, World Bank, USAID, and UNDP.

Our Mandate

What Planning Cell Does

Scheme Appraisal & Approval

The Cell reviews, appraises, and recommends developmental schemes from all 26 District Administrations before forwarding to the Board of Revenue and P&D Department for formal approval under PDWP/DDWP frameworks.

ADP Coordination

Planning Cell prepares and coordinates the Annual Development Programme (ADP) for Revenue & Estate Department, ensuring district-level priorities are aligned with provincial development goals and resource allocations.

Monitoring & Evaluation

Regular monitoring visits to all districts to track physical and financial progress of developmental projects. The Cell ensures compliance with approved PC-I/PC-II documents and timely completion of schemes.

Project Reporting

Timely compilation and submission of quarterly progress reports to Board of Revenue, P&D Department, and other relevant authorities. Maintains institutional memory through comprehensive documentation.

Capacity Development

Coordinating training programs for planning officers across District Administrations. Facilitating foreign trainings and knowledge-sharing sessions to enhance institutional capacity in project management.

Digital Reform Coordination

Coordinating with Directorate of Land Records on digitization of land records, e-registration rollout, SDC expansion, and GIS-based land settlement across KP districts including newly merged districts.

Planning & Policy

Annual Development Programme 2026-27

In pursuance of the Rules of Business of the Government of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, the Planning Cell is mandated to prepare, coordinate and oversee the Annual Development Programme (ADP) for the Revenue & Estate Department — encompassing all developmental schemes of District Administrations across the province.

"The Planning Cell serves as the institutional hub for developmental governance — bridging district-level administration with the provincial revenue framework to ensure accountable, transparent and result-oriented public investment across KP."

ADP 2026-27 has been formulated following extensive consultative meetings with all Commissioners, Deputy Commissioners, and Line Departments under the chairmanship of the Senior Member BOR.

DDWP Approval

Schemes up to Rs. 40 million approved at District Development Working Party level.

PDWP Approval

Larger schemes forwarded to Provincial Development Working Party with full PC-I documentation.

PC-I / PC-II

Full project appraisal documents prepared and reviewed by Planning Cell before submission.

PSDP Projects

Federal PSDP projects monitored and coordinated in liaison with NEC and Planning Commission.

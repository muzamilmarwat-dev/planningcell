About Planning Cell

Planning Cell
Revenue & Estate Department

The Planning Cell, Revenue & Estate Department is a dedicated institutional unit established under the Board of Revenue, Government of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Its core mandate is to oversee, plan, coordinate, and monitor all developmental schemes undertaken by the District Administrations across the 26 districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

In pursuance of the Rules of Business of the Government of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, the Planning Cell operates as the principal planning authority within the Revenue & Estate Department, interfacing with district-level administrations, the Board of Revenue, and other provincial departments to ensure aligned and effective public investment.

The Cell is responsible for the preparation, appraisal, approval, execution oversight, and evaluation of developmental schemes — ensuring transparency, fiscal discipline, and delivery of public services to citizens across KP.

Our Policy

Planning Cell Policy

In pursuance of the Rules of Business of the Government of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, the Planning Cell, Revenue & Estate Department is mandated to plan, coordinate and oversee the developmental schemes of the District Administrations of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

"The Planning Cell serves as the institutional hub for developmental governance — bridging district-level administration with the provincial revenue framework to ensure accountable, transparent and result-oriented public investment across KP."

Development Areas

Sectoral Focus

Agriculture Sector

Agriculture

Enabling the Province to meet challenges of food security by developing a vibrant agricultural sector that promotes productivity and value addition.

Building Sector

Buildings & Works

Constructing and maintaining residential and office accommodation facilities in the public sector to ensure conducive environment for public service delivery.

Energy Sector

Energy & Power

Developing the province's potential in Hydel Power Generation and Alternate Energy to contribute significantly in mitigating energy crisis.

Education Sector

Education

Improving educational infrastructure and access across districts, with focus on primary and secondary schools in underserved areas of KP.

Health Sector

Health

Providing adequate healthcare infrastructure and services delivery through BHUs, RHCs and District Headquarter Hospitals across KP.

DWSS Sector

Water & Sanitation

Providing adequate and safe water supply and extending sanitation coverage to un-served and underserved areas across the province.

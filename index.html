About the Planning Cell

Revenue & Estate Department's
Planning Cell

The Planning Cell, Revenue & Estate Department is a dedicated unit functioning under the Board of Revenue, Government of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. It was established with the specific mandate to overlook, plan, and facilitate the developmental schemes undertaken by District Administrations across all districts of KP.

The Cell acts as the institutional bridge between district-level development initiatives and the overarching revenue administration framework. It ensures that all developmental projects align with provincial priorities, regulatory standards, and fiscal prudence.

From project identification, appraisal, and approval to monitoring and evaluation, the Planning Cell provides end-to-end oversight — ensuring transparency, accountability, and effective utilisation of public resources.

Our Work

Developmental Schemes

The Planning Cell coordinates, monitors, and evaluates a broad portfolio of developmental schemes across Khyber Pakhtunkhwa's district administrations.

Infrastructure Development

Construction and rehabilitation of district-level roads, buildings, and public facilities to strengthen administrative capacity.

Ongoing

Land Record Digitisation

Modernising revenue records through digital platforms, ensuring transparency, accuracy, and public access to land data.

Priority Scheme

Capacity Building Programmes

Training and professional development of district revenue staff to enhance planning, monitoring, and administrative skills.

Ongoing

Community Development Initiatives

Small-scale community-driven projects designed to address grassroots needs with the support of District Administrations.

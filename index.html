Mr. Zahir Shah

Senior Member Board of Revenue — Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

The Revenue & Estate Department, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa has undertaken a transformative initiative for the computerization of land records across the province, aimed at improving service delivery and enhancing public convenience. Under this initiative, modern technologies are being incorporated into the land record management system to ensure efficiency, transparency, and accuracy.

The integration of digitized land records into a centralized database will enable real-time updates from a single platform and allow seamless connectivity with various service gateways. At the same time, manual settlement of land records continues in several districts to ensure completeness and reliability of data.

In addition, a GIS-based land settlement approach has recently been introduced in seven sub-divisions of the newly merged districts to develop fully digitized and spatially accurate records. A similar settlement initiative is also underway in the districts of Lower Dir, Upper Dir, and Sub-Tehsil Kalam in District Swat.

Mr. Basharat Ahmad

Director Land Record — Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

At the Directorate of Land Records, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, our core objective is to establish a modern, secure, and fully digitized land administration system that promotes transparency, efficiency, and citizen-focused service delivery across the province.

We are implementing advanced solutions such as end-to-end e-registration, GIS-based cadastral mapping, and integrated Service Delivery Centers (SDCs) to ensure timely and reliable access to land-related services. The integration of all SDCs into a centralized monitoring system has significantly improved oversight, performance tracking, and service efficiency.

Under Phase-I of the Land Record Computerization Project, all seven districts at divisional headquarters have been successfully digitized. Building on this progress, Phase-II aims to complete digitization in thirteen additional districts by 30th June 2025.

To further enhance transparency and reduce manual intervention, e-registration is being introduced, enabling citizens to process land registrations with greater ease, accuracy, and minimal dependency on intermediaries.

Our commitment extends to strengthening governance frameworks, ensuring data integrity, minimizing discretionary practices, and deploying real-time monitoring dashboards for effective field management. Public convenience remains at the forefront of our reforms, with improvements in service environments, digital payment systems, and streamlined, trackable workflows.

We actively encourage feedback and suggestions from the public, as continuous improvement in service delivery remains our highest priority.

About Planning Cell

Planning Cell
Revenue & Estate Department

The Planning Cell, Revenue & Estate Department is a dedicated institutional unit established under the Board of Revenue, Government of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Its core mandate is to oversee, plan, coordinate, and monitor all developmental schemes undertaken by the District Administrations across the 26 districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

In pursuance of the Rules of Business of the Government of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, the Planning Cell operates as the principal planning authority within the Revenue & Estate Department, interfacing with district-level administrations, the Board of Revenue, and other provincial departments to ensure aligned and effective public investment.

The Cell is responsible for the preparation, appraisal, approval, execution oversight, and evaluation of developmental schemes — ensuring transparency, fiscal discipline, and delivery of public services to citizens across KP.
